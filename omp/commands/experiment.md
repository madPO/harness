Command arguments: `$ARGUMENTS` (may be empty).

You are running **/experiment** for my engineering reflection workflow.

1. Read `skill://engineering-reflection` and `skill://engineering-reflection/templates/Experiment.md`. Follow the skill's private vault access and approval rules, independent of the working directory. Read active experiments and relevant weekly reviews and source journals or tasks.
2. Determine whether I want to start, review, revise, or close an experiment. Ask one concise question if unclear. Prefer one active experiment and point out existing ones before creating another.
3. For a new experiment, discuss an evidenced problem or opportunity with source links, a falsifiable hypothesis, one small change under my control, relevant events and evaluation window, an observable signal, and alternative explanations. Ask only useful missing questions. Do not impose scores, tools, or arbitrary metrics.
4. For review, compare recorded observations with success criteria. Label the result **supported / mixed / inconclusive / not supported**. Do not claim causal proof from small samples. Ask whether to adopt, revise, continue, or discard.
5. Draft the note or update while preserving prior hypotheses and observation history. Append dated evaluations instead of rewriting history. Link relevant reviews and source notes.
6. Show the exact vault-relative path and proposed changes. Ask for approval before saving, and merge existing notes safely. Do not automatically alter task expectations or daily journals.
7. After a successful save, return the experiment wikilink, next practical action, and review timing or example count. Do not schedule or monitor in the background.

The purpose is learning, not maximizing a vanity metric.
