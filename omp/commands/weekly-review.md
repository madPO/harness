Command arguments: `$ARGUMENTS` (may be empty).

You are running **/weekly-review** for my engineering reflection workflow.

1. Read `skill://engineering-reflection` and `skill://engineering-reflection/templates/Weekly.md`. Follow the skill's private vault access and approval rules, independent of the working directory. Determine the local ISO Monday–Sunday week from the argument or current week. State its dates and mark an unfinished week provisional.
2. Read that week's journals and relevant linked tasks, including frozen expectations and outcomes. Read active experiments and the previous weekly review when useful. Check this month's prior weekly reviews for recognition candidates and action status; avoid duplicates.
3. Build an evidence index of recorded facts. Never infer unlogged activity from missing notes. Separate observations, hypotheses, and open questions; link actual notes.
4. Review evidenced delivery, estimation surprises, blockers and necessary versus avoidable rework; engineering quality and tradeoffs; professional growth; and collaboration, appreciation, and feedback opportunities. Story points are team-planning context, not a personal score.
5. For each recognition candidate, include person, specific contribution, impact, date or link, and suggested next step. Check whether already thanked, nominated, or dismissed. Recognition is optional, not a quota. Address behavior or process, not motives or character. Never draft or send a message unless asked.
6. Identify up to three plausible recurring patterns, citing notes, alternative explanations, and gaps. Highlight a successful practice to repeat when supported.
7. Suggest one small experiment with problem, hypothesis, action, evaluation window or event count, observable signal, and caveats. If an experiment is active, favor reviewing or continuing it.
8. Present a compact draft and ask one targeted question if useful. Request approval before writing the exact vault-relative review path. Reuse existing reviews and preserve edits.
9. After saving, return the wikilink, top insight, suggested experiment, and optional appreciation or feedback reminder. Create or change an experiment only through `/experiment` or with separate explicit approval. Never submit recognition or contact colleagues.

If evidence is insufficient, say so, offer a short provisional review, and ask about missing meaningful outcomes or interactions.
